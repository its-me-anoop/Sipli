## Review notes

Sipli 6.2 is a hydration tracker with an Apple Watch companion and widgets. This update improves responsive hydration insights, gentle bottle encouragement and achievement sharing. It also improves coffee colour contrast and removes implementation labels from ordinary app screens. There is no camera recognition or real-bottle inventory feature in this build.

No account or demo login is required. Complete onboarding and use the normal Add flow to log water. The bottle reflects remaining daily-goal progress; it does not measure a physical bottle. Log a drink, undo or delete an entry, or change the daily goal to check refreshed progress and insights. Encouragement is dismissible, respects Reduce Motion, and is limited so it does not interrupt entry flows or appear repeatedly. Welcome-back feedback requires a logging gap and may not appear immediately on a fresh installation.
