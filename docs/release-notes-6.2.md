# Sipli 6.2 (23)

Bottle Companion is an optional way to track the real water bottle beside you. Set its capacity, then match the remaining waterline to log only what you drank. Top-ups and discarded or shared water adjust contents without adding intake. Finish logs only what remains; undo restores the last bottle action, including after relaunch or midnight.

Quantities are estimates. The daily-goal bottle and existing drink logging stay available separately. Track a bottle through one logging flow to avoid counting the same drink twice.
