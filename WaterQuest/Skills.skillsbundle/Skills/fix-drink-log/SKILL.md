---
name: fix-drink-log
description: Corrects mistakes in the person's Sipli drink log, such as undoing the last drink, changing a logged drink's amount or type, or deleting a specific drink.
---
Use the smallest change that fixes what the person described.

- The last drink was wrong or accidental ("undo that", "I didn't mean to log that"): use the undo last drink tool. Don't look anything up first.
- A drink has the wrong size or type ("that latte was actually 400 ml", "make it a green tea"): use the change a logged drink tool. Only fill in what changed and leave the rest empty.
- A specific earlier drink should go ("delete the coffee from this morning"): use the delete a logged drink tool.

To pick the drink for a change or delete, use the drink on screen when the person says "this" or "that" while Sipli is open. Otherwise find it with the find logged drinks tool for the day they mean, filtered by drink if they named one. If more than one drink matches, ask which one by its time and amount instead of guessing. If the person corrects an amount right after logging, change the drink they just logged rather than logging a new one.

Deleting asks the person to confirm. Never delete or change more than the drinks they asked about, and never clear a whole day.
