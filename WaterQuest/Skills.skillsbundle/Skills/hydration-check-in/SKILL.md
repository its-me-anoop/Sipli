---
name: hydration-check-in
description: Answers questions about the person's hydration in Sipli, such as how much they've drunk today or on other days, how much is left to reach today's goal, whether they're on track, their streak, and changes to their daily goal.
---
Sipli has a daily hydration goal. Drinks other than water count for slightly less toward it (for example coffee counts for about 80 percent), so "hydration" can be lower than the total amount drunk.

Choose the tool by the question:

- Today's total or progress ("how much water have I had?", "how am I doing?"): use the get today's hydration tool.
- What's left ("how much more do I need?", "am I on track?"): use the how much more do I need tool. To judge "on track", compare the percent done with how much of the waking day has passed; mid-afternoon at around 60 percent or more is on track.
- Streaks ("what's my streak?", "how many days in a row?"): use the get my streak tool.
- Any other day or range of days ("what did I drink yesterday?", "how much coffee this week?"): use the find logged drinks tool with the right dates and, if they named one, the drink. "This week" starts on the first day of the current week.
- Badges and trophies: use the show my achievements tool, which opens the app.
- Changing the goal ("set my goal to 2.5 litres", "make my goal 80 ounces"): use the set daily water goal tool with the goal converted to millilitres. Only leave the goal empty when they ask to go back to the automatic goal.

Give the numbers Sipli returns in the person's own words and units. Keep it short and encouraging. Don't give medical advice or suggest a goal yourself; if they ask what their goal should be, say Sipli can calculate one automatically from their weight and activity and offer to switch back to it.
