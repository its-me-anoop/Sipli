---
name: log-drinks
description: Logs water, coffee, tea or any other drinks to the person's Sipli hydration log, including several drinks at once, drinks from earlier today or this week, and "my usual".
---
Sipli tracks what a person drinks against a daily hydration goal. Pick the logging tool that fits what they said:

- One drink with a size ("log 350 ml of water", "I had a 12 oz coffee"): use the log an amount tool, or the log water tool with an amount and fluid type.
- One named drink with no size ("log a latte"): use the log a drink tool. Sipli fills in the size they usually have, so don't ask how much.
- "My usual", "same again", "another one": use the log my usual tool.
- Several drinks, or a drink with a time ("two coffees and a water this morning", "I had tea at 9"): use the log drinks tool once, with one item per drink. "Two coffees" is two coffee items. Put each drink's own time on it, and leave the time empty for drinks had just now.

Amounts are in millilitres. Convert anything else before calling a tool: 1 US fl oz is about 30 mL, a cup about 240 mL, a mug about 350 mL, a pint about 470 mL, a bottle of water about 500 mL, a litre 1000 mL. If the person didn't give a size, leave the amount empty rather than guessing; Sipli uses their usual size.

Pick the closest drink type rather than "other": a flat white is flat white, an iced latte is iced coffee or latte, fizzy water is sparkling water, OJ is juice. Use water for a plain glass, bottle or cup of water.

Don't log anything the person only asked about ("should I have another coffee?"), and don't log the same drink twice if they repeat themselves to correct an amount; change the drink they just logged instead. Sipli can only add drinks from the last 7 days.

After logging, Sipli replies with progress toward today's goal. Pass that on briefly; don't add your own hydration advice.
